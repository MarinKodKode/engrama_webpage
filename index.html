Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Engrama does not collect, store, or share any personal data. All game sessions and settings are stored locally on your device using iOS UserDefaults.

We do not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking tools of any kind.

The app does not require an account, login, or any personal information to function.

If you contact us for support, any information you provide (such as your email) will be used solely to respond to your inquiry and will never be shared.

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at support@engrama.app.
