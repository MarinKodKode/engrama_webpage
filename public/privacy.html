🔒 Privacy First

Privacy Policy

We built Engrama to train your brain, not to collect your data. Here's everything you need to know — in plain language.

🙅

We don't collect your data. Period.

No account required. No tracking. No analytics. No ads.
Everything stays on your device.

Last updated: April 29, 2026

📦

Data Collection

Engrama does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers.

  • All game sessions are stored locally on your device
  • All settings and preferences are stored locally on your device
  • Your profile name and division are stored locally on your device
  • None of this data ever leaves your iPhone
📊

Analytics & Tracking

We do not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.

  • No Firebase Analytics
  • No Mixpanel, Amplitude, or similar tools
  • No advertising identifiers (IDFA)
  • No crash reporting that identifies you personally
🔗

Third-Party Services

Engrama does not integrate with any third-party services that collect user data. The app works entirely offline — no internet connection is required to play.

👶

Children's Privacy

Engrama is designed for students of all ages, including children under 13. Since we do not collect any personal data, there are no special requirements or restrictions regarding children's use of the app.

Parents and teachers can use Engrama with full confidence that no personal information about their students is ever collected or shared.

🗑️

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by:

  • Deleting the Engrama app from your iPhone
  • Going to Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Engrama → Delete App

This will permanently remove all locally stored data.

✉️

Support Communications

If you contact us for support via email, any information you voluntarily provide — such as your name or email address — will be used solely to respond to your inquiry.

We will never share, sell, or use this information for any other purpose.

Contact us at: kodkode.support@gmail.com

📝

Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this privacy policy, the new version will be posted at this URL and the "Last updated" date will be changed. We will notify users of any significant changes through an app update.
