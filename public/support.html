Frequently Asked Questions

Is Engrama free?

+

Yes, Engrama is completely free to download and play. There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or ads.

Does Engrama work offline?

+

Yes, 100%. Engrama works entirely offline. No internet connection is needed to play any of the three challenges.

My progress disappeared. What happened?

+

All data is stored locally on your device. If you deleted and reinstalled the app, your data was removed along with it. Unfortunately this cannot be recovered yet — cloud backup is planned for a future update.

How do I move up in Division?

+

Your Division is based on your total accumulated points across all three game modes — Math, Language, and Trivia. Every point from every session counts. Keep playing and you'll automatically climb from Plutón all the way to Sol.

Is Engrama available on Android?

+

Not yet, but an Android version is currently in development. Follow us on GitHub to stay updated on the progress.

Can I use Engrama in my classroom?

+

Absolutely — Engrama was designed with students in mind. It's completely safe for children of all ages, requires no account, and collects zero personal data. Teachers love it for mental math and vocabulary practice.

How do I share my results?

+

Go to your Analytics screen, tap on any session in your history, and press "Compartir resultado". A high-resolution card with your score and Division will be generated and you can share it to Instagram, WhatsApp, or any other app.
